Our Goal

Pacific Trails Resort was created to preserve the ancient footpaths once traveled by local coastal communities. Our goal is to maintain these historic trail routes while providing zero-impact accommodations that honor the history of the land.

Visit Us

The trails surrounding our resort connect miles of undisturbed coastline, tide pools, and pine forests. Walking these headlands offers guests a direct connection to California's rich natural history, framed by panoramic Pacific views and protected ocean air.

Core Values

  • Sustaining and preserving our ocean ecosystems
  • Encouraging curiosity and outdoor adventure for all ages
  • Creating quiet spaces for mindfulness and rejuvenation
  • Celebrating local coastal cuisine and community gathering
